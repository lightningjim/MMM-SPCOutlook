<objective>
Produce concrete UAT fixtures from live ERO data, then run the human verification pass that closes the phase against its five success criteria.

Task 1 removes that ambiguity by deriving real coordinates and expected values from the live layers immediately before the human runs the check. Task 2 is the checkpoint.

Output: `14-UAT-FIXTURES.md` in the phase directory, plus a human sign-off covering all five ROADMAP success criteria.

- **The HIGH tier is verified structurally, not by live observation.** Neither research session observed a live `dn: 4` feature; the legend entry is schema-confirmed from the layer's own `drawingInfo.renderer`. Per RESEARCH.md Open Question 1, HIGH's correctness is asserted by source assertion (plan 14-01's `toValue`/`valueToTier` checks proved `dn: 4` maps to `HIGH` maps to `"High"`), and the UAT does **not** block on finding a live High-risk day. - **UAT does not require an active severe-weather day.** Criteria 1, 2, 4, and 5 are exercisable in any weather. Criterion 3 requires an inside-polygon location, which ERO almost always has *somewhere* in CONUS — Task 1 finds one wherever it currently is, rather than waiting for risk to appear over the user's home coordinates.
- **Fixtures are generated fresh at execution time, not copied from research.** ERO polygons move every issuance cycle. A coordinate captured on 2026-08-18 may be outside every polygon by the time the human runs the check, which would produce a false failure of criterion 3.

   - Confirm no logged URL contains `f=json` without the `geo` prefix.
