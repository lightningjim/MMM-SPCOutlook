<objective>
Replace `getSpcOutlook`'s three named new-product awaits and its `kml-advisory` for-loop with one
six-member `Promise.allSettled` batch, add D-10's per-member timing log, and mechanically verify the
two shared helper-global fields survive intra-run concurrency.

Purpose: PERF-01. On a stock config the batch has one live member and five toggle-off no-ops — the
benefit is realised as products are enabled, which is exactly how the requirement is worded. MPD's
discovery-then-N-KMZ chain is the longest single sequence in the set, so overlapping it is where
most of the wall-clock win actually is.

`Promise.allSettled` rather than `Promise.all` is locked (D-08) because every runner is only
*documented* to never throw. `Promise.all` would convert that comment into a load-bearing invariant
and let one throw discard five healthy payloads into `getSpcOutlook`'s own catch — the "total outage
rendering as a confident all-clear" shape that was Phase 14's highest-severity round-3 finding. A
latency fix must not buy speed with a new outage class.

Output: `node_helper.js:2905-2965` restructured; no other region touched.
</objective>

    async functions can only interleave at an `await` boundary. None of the five sites
