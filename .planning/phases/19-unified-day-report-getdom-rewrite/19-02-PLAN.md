<objective>
Thread the SPC proximity subtree into Phase 18's unified `days[]` grid.

**Why this plan exists — a gap neither RESEARCH.md nor PATTERNS.md caught.** Verified by source
read at planning time: `proximity` exists **only** on the legacy `day1`/`day2`/`day3` payload
blocks (`node_helper.js:5109-5145`, assembled by `buildProximitySubtree` at `:4206-4214`). It is
absent from `days[n]`, from `days[n].hazards[]`, and from the convective `detail` sub-object
(`_addSpcGridEntries`, `:3159-3167`). CONTEXT.md D-07 (proximity badges on the convective
sub-row and per-hazard-type badges on the probabilistic sub-line) and D-08 (a proximity-only day
renders in compact) both require proximity, and RPT-06 parity requires PROXUI-01..05 survive —
none of which is reachable from the unified payload today.

The renderer must read only `this.spcrisk.days` / `.summary` / `.sources` / `.advisories`
(PROJECT.md: unified day report is the sole render path, no legacy fallback). Therefore the data
must move, and this is the plan that moves it. This is not new product, fetch, or merge logic —
it relocates an already-computed value into the payload shape the new renderer reads.

Purpose: unblock RPT-03's detail sub-rows and RPT-06's PROXUI parity rows.
Output: `days[n].proximity` present for grid days 1-3, mutation-proven by three probe scenarios.
</objective>
