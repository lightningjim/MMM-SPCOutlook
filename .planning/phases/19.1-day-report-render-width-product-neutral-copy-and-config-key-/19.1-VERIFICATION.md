report: the detail-mode day report no longer widens its MagicMirror region or overlaps neighbouring

## Summary Up Front

**The blocking limb-1 gap is closed.** The two truths this phase previously FAILED — that the
detail-mode day report no longer overlaps the centre column, and that the region cap is correct for
the deployed hardware — are now positively confirmed on the deployed Raspberry Pi, against the same
19-UAT test 5 baseline screenshot, under deploy-validity preconditions that were independently
confirmed rather than assumed. The other eight truths are re-checked at HEAD and show no regression;
two of the previous report's minor incidental findings are also resolved.

**What closed it was a mechanism replacement, not a number.** Plan 19.1-06 retired the monospace
character-advance/`min-width:Nch` column mechanism in favour of CSS grid tracks
(`display:inline-grid` with `minmax(0,Nfr)` columns) and re-derived the cap in the opposite order —
available space first (`1020 − 540 − 30 = 450px` → `REGION_CAP_REM = 22.5` at the measured 20px
root), content allocated inside that budget afterwards. The previous report's finding that the
*derivation method* was disproven, not merely the number, is upheld by what actually fixed it: the
replacement value came from the reversed order, and no cap tuning was needed or applied
(19.1-09 shipped a zero code diff).

**Status is `human_needed`, not `passed`, and the distinction matters here.** There are zero gaps
and zero blocking items; the phase goal is achieved and the phase is ready to close. Four items
remain that no automated check and no observation to date has exercised — most importantly
**item 5, co-equal hazards rendering as peers, which is NOT OBSERVABLE** (no co-equal hazard pair
was live at the OKC coordinate). NOT OBSERVABLE is neither a pass nor a failure, and it is
deliberately **not** folded into the operator's "Approved for all" disposition. Recording it as an
open observation rather than absorbing it into a blanket approval is the whole point.

### Claim Strength — Stated Precisely, Neither Inflated Nor Deflated

This is the section the phase's own discipline exists for, so it is stated plainly.

**What was actually observed (truth 1):** a human looked at the deployed mirror, with
`dayReportDetail: true` genuinely active, holding the 19-UAT test 5 baseline screenshot, and stated
that the day-report block does not cross into the centre column. That is an **operator visual
observation against a baseline**, not a pixel measurement of the running Electron renderer.

**What was NOT re-measured:** the predicted left edge at **x ≈ 570** (30px right of the x=540
centreline) is a **derivation** from the panel geometry measured on 2026-09-10 — `1020 − 450 = 570`.
Nothing re-measured 570px on screen this run. Any statement that the region "was measured at x=570"
would be false, and neither the live-check record nor this report makes it.

**Why the observation is nonetheless strong, and not a rubber stamp:**

1. **The instrument demonstrably discriminates.** The identical protocol, identical question wording,
   identical baseline screenshot and identical operator returned **"Yes, it still extends into the
   centre column"** twenty-four hours earlier and produced a blocking FAIL. An instrument that
   returned a FAIL on the broken build and a PASS on the fixed build is doing the job. This is the
   single strongest argument against dismissing the approval as agreeable acquiescence.
2. **The failure being tested for was gross, not marginal.** The disproven cap overshot the
   centreline by 168px — roughly a third of the region's width. Distinguishing a 168px overlap from
   no overlap is exactly the kind of categorical judgement human vision is reliable at; it would be
   a different matter if the claim were "clears the centreline by 30px", which is NOT what was
   observed and NOT what this report records.
3. **The claim the requirement makes is categorical.** 19-UAT test 5 asks whether the block overlaps
   the neighbouring column. It does not specify a pixel budget. The categorical outcome observed
   **is** the claim the requirement states — so this is not a weaker proxy for the requirement, it
   is the requirement, confirmed at the strongest strength available without instrumenting the
   running renderer.
4. **Attribution is airtight and independently re-verified.** The observation is attributable to
   exactly this source: deployed `sha256 = e951fb51…` equals `git show HEAD:MMM-SPCOutlook.js`,
   re-checked by this verification directly on the Pi, with the retired mechanism's markers
   (`32.4rem`, `DejaVu Sans Mono`) both `0` on the **deployed** file. Both failure modes that
   invalidated the 19-08 observation (stale deploy; misspelled `dayReportDetails` key) are provably
   excluded.
5. **The complementary bound was also observed.** Item 3 (no wrapping anywhere) independently rules
   out the opposite error — a cap set so tight it merely hides the overlap by reflowing content.
   A PASS on item 1 alone would have been compatible with an over-tight cap; PASS on 1 *and* 3 is
   not.

**What remains weaker than the record might suggest, stated adversarially:**

- **The two remote-side facts I could re-verify, I did** (digest, markers, config restoration). The
  **ten operator verdicts I could not** — they are human statements recorded in `19.1-LIVE-CHECK-2.md`
  and this report takes them at their stated strength. There is no mechanism by which a verifier can
  re-derive them; noting this is honesty, not doubt.
- **The observation window was narrow.** One restart, one payload, one coordinate (OKC), one hazard
  population, one time of day. The cap is a static style assignment so this is a small risk, but the
  content that had to fit inside it on 2026-09-11 was a single-hazard day — a lighter load than a
  busy convective day would present. Item 3's "no wrapping" is therefore a weaker guarantee of
  headroom than it would be on a heavy day. This is the most substantive residual weakness in the
  limb-1 claim and it is not recorded anywhere else in the phase's documents.
- **Production does not currently run the tested configuration.** `dayReportDetail` was restored to
  `false` (correctly — restoration was owed and this verification confirmed it was performed). The
  defect's trigger condition is therefore not active on the live mirror, so no ongoing passive
  observation will catch a regression here.
- **The layout claim is and remains permanently MANUAL ONLY.** Verified: `19.1-UI-SPEC.md`'s
  Verification Honesty rows 5 and 6 (lines 576-577) both still read **`NO — MANUAL ONLY`** and each
  carries an explicit gap-closure addendum stating the classification "does NOT change and must never
  change… Reclassifying this row on the strength of a passing live check is the precise error that
  would let the next regression through." `grep -c 'MANUAL ONLY'` = 2, unchanged. **No defect found
  here** — the honesty rows survived a passing live check without being softened, which is the exact
  outcome required.
